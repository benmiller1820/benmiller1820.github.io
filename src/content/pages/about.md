## How I got here

Cognitive science taught me to look at how people actually think, not how we assume they do. I became a product manager because I love understanding people as much as I enjoy solving problems. After cognitive science at Penn and an MBA at Duke's Fuqua School of Business, I found product to be the perfect blend of psychology, technology, and business strategy.

## What I believe

> AI is making products more powerful and more complicated. Someone has to make sure people can keep up.

If no one looks out for the person on the other end, the gap between what technology can do and what people can use grows with every release. Good product management closes it by shaping complicated technology around how people actually think. That's why Clink skips the long intake survey: most homeowners can't answer technical questions about their repair, and shouldn't have to.
